pstack ships 58 skills. This pack carries eleven of them, plus its 24
principles folded into one indexed skill rather than 24 separate ones — a
skill's description occupies the context window for the whole session, so the
cost of carrying a skill is paid on every turn whether or not it is used. The
ones left out are either narrower than this pack (`ketchup-video`,
`make-bot-ui`), specific to one stack (`typescript-best-practices`), or about
operating an agent fleet rather than engineering (`swarm`, `arena`,
`setup-pstack`).
