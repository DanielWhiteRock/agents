## What we changed

MIT does not require a change statement. Listed anyway, so a buyer can tell our
edits from the authors' work.

| File | Change |
|---|---|
| `skills/landing-page-conversion-audit/SKILL.md` | Removed the closing "Implementing the fixes" section, which recommended a specific third-party funnel-builder project and walked through installing it. We are not going to sell a pack that promotes somebody's product inside the method. Also removed a one-clause pointer to a sibling tracking skill that does not travel with this pack. Nothing else in the audit procedure was touched. |

The three `gtm-*` skills are byte-for-byte upstream, including their "Related
Skills" sections, which name sibling skills from the same author that this pack
does not carry.

## Why these four

The role is "finds who would buy it, and audits the page that has to sell it" —
evidence in, sellable deliverable out. Positioning decides which buyer shows up;
account planning qualifies the one in front of you and says honestly when the
deal is dead; the 0-to-1 skill is for a product with no customers yet; and the
conversion audit is the page half, ordered by revenue moved rather than by what
is easy to check.

Left out on purpose: `competitor-ad-intelligence` and the rest of the paid-media
material. The catalogue's paid-media shelf is 18 listings with zero recorded
uses, and we are not stocking a shelf nobody shops.

What this pack deliberately does not do is campaign planning — channel plans,
calendars, budget splits. That was the half of the old Marketer role the
evidence could not support, and the pack says so rather than quietly implying
otherwise.

The composition is ours; the skills are theirs.
